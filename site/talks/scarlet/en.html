Neutrinos, antineutrinos
and inelasticity

Structure-function model dependence of the inelasticity of TeV–PeV neutrino interactions and of neutrino–antineutrino separation in neutrino telescopes

Rafael C. R. de Lima

with Bruno Duarte da Silva Moreira

UDESC/CCT · Joinville, Brasil

rafael-lima.pages.dev/talks
This work follows how the structure-function prescription affects the inferred composition. The central distinction is between describing the mean inelasticity and stably recovering the neutrino-to-antineutrino ratio. Manuscript in preparation, 30 October 2026 version. The palette draws on the Interstellar reference supplied by the author.

Main question

Similar means, different compositions

How much does the inferred ν/ν̄ composition change when we change the interaction templates?

The shift depends on both the template difference and the remaining separating power.

We do not measure a bias in IceCube. We fit one model expectation with another model templates and control the differences.

The observatory

A cubic kilometre of instrumented ice

5,160 optical modules on 86 strings.

Sensors at depths of 1,450–2,450 m.

Charged particles produce Cherenkov light. Signal times and intensities constrain the event geometry and energy.

Charged-current interactions

The cascade and muon share the energy

νμ + N → μ− + X     ν̄μ + N → μ+ + X

The hadronic cascade receives approximately yEν. The muon receives (1 − y)Eν.

The detector measures light and reconstructs energies. The μ−/μ+ charge is unavailable event by event.

IceCube, Phys. Rev. D 99 (2019) 032004 · arXiv:1808.07629
These are interaction-level relations. In the detector, the cascade visible energy depends on hadronic response and the muon may leave the instrumented volume. Visible y therefore differs from true y.

The domain of Bjorken x and Q²

Q² = 2M Eν xy     W² = M² + Q²(1/x − 1)

x measures the nucleon momentum fraction sampled by the interaction. Q² sets the resolution scale.

Bias mechanism

Separation enters the denominator

δf ≃ − [(1−f)δν + fδν̄] / [⟨y⟩ν − ⟨y⟩ν̄]

δν and δν̄ are the true mean shifts relative to the template means.

As separation decreases, a small shape mismatch can shift the composition substantially. The full fit uses the entire distribution.

Manuscript · eq. (2.4)
This relation applies to a mean-based estimate. The numerical results use a binned likelihood and also respond to shape differences at similar means. The approximation’s divergence does not imply infinite sensitivity.

Dipole description

The boson fluctuates into a quark pair

FT,L = Q²/(4π²) Σq₁q̄₂ ∫ d²r ∫ dz DT,LV(z,r) σqq̄(x̃,r)

Current wave functions determine the coupling to the pair. The dipole cross section describes its interaction with the target.

BGK includes DGLAP evolution of the gluon. This work uses a HERA fit and three matching prescriptions.

BGK, Phys. Rev. D 66 (2002) 014001 · Manuscript §3
Here r is the dipole size, not the flux ratio. FL comes from longitudinal polarisation and xF3 from the transverse-polarisation difference. The c sbar fluctuation has unequal masses. We do not attribute the results to saturation without a linearised-dipole comparison.

Interpretation limits

What the results establish

1

Differences between prescriptions

The family is finite and shares inputs. Its envelope has no probabilistic coverage.

2

Complete configurations in the detector

Nuclear and normalisation effects have not been separated at this level.

3

Inference in a proxy

The tests do not establish a bias in IceCube’s measurement or validate its real response.

These limits define the result supported today. Alternative valence and matching prescriptions and a linearised dipole can extend the controls, but the conclusions do not require attributing the shift to saturation.
